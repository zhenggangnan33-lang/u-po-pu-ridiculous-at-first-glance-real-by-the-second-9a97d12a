A one-sentence startup pitch for the make.fun bounty
[Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second](https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-ridiculous-at-first-glance-real-by-the-second-9a97d12a):
a bizarre idea that sounds like a joke until the business logic lands.

## Entry

> We rent trained ravens to deliver breakup letters, because a bird dropping 'it is over' on your ex's balcony is unforgettable, and the newly single pay $49 a drop to never have that conversation.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
when that is set). The text is a single sentence of 195 characters, within
the competition's 1–200 character gate, and `main.ts` validates that length
before writing, failing with a clear error if the gate would reject it.
